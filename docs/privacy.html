Crease · Cricket Scoring

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Developer: AbdurRehmanBinWaheed · Package: app.awventures.crease

1. Overview

Crease (developed by AbdurRehmanBinWaheed) is an offline-first cricket scoring and tournament management app. We collect only the minimum data needed to sync your matches, teams, and career stats across devices. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party advertising.

2. Information We Collect

  • Guest Mode (Offline Only): You can score matches and manage local teams without creating an account. In Guest Mode, all data stays locally on your device.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you sign in with Email or Google Sign-In, we store your email address, display name, playing role, and a unique user ID.
  • Photos (Optional): If you choose to upload a profile photo or team logo from your camera or gallery, that image is stored in Supabase Storage and linked to your profile or team.
  • Cricket & Match Data: Teams, squad lists, tournaments, and ball-by-ball match events you create or join so that scorecards, points tables, NRR, and player stats stay synced.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and back up your teams, matches, and tournaments.
  • Link your account to your player spot on a team via captain invite codes.
  • Generate shareable live scorecards and career stat summaries when you choose to share them.

4. Third-Party Services & Security

Crease uses Supabase (authentication, PostgreSQL database, and file storage) and optionally Google Sign-In for authentication. All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

5. Shared & Public Data

When you share a live match link, tournament link, or team profile code, anyone with that link or code can view the corresponding match scorecard, standings, or public team roster. Your email address is never displayed publicly.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Crease account at any time directly inside the app:

  1. Open Crease and tap your avatar to open Profile.
  2. Scroll to Account & backup and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

When you delete your account:

  • Your authentication account, profile metadata, and uploaded photos (avatars and team logos) are permanently deleted immediately.
  • Your account is unlinked from any claimed team player spots (user_id and avatar cleared).
  • Local app data on your device is cleared.
  • Shared match scorecards and tournament records remain intact for teammates without any link to your account.

If you no longer have access to the app and want your account deleted, email AbdurRehmanBinWaheed at arehman.celoks@gmail.com from your registered email address and we will complete your request within 7 days. Unlinked shared match scorecards are retained as part of teammates' match history with no personal account identifiers attached.

7. Children’s Privacy

Crease is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us to have it removed.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
