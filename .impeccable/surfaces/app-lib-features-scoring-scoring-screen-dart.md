# Live Scoring Screen

- **Scope:** Primary Quick Match live ball-by-ball scoring screen (`app/lib/features/scoring/scoring_screen.dart`), plus Quick Match setup, full scorecard, and shareable summary.
- **Mode:** Operate
- **Approved Comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/comp-b-open-sheet.jpg`

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Open-air monumental score directly on a cool near-white daylight ground paired with a raised frosted-glass one-thumb scoring sheet, refusing the dark-navy-and-neon-card sports template that washes out in sunlight.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Daylight Glass — `#EEF3F3` cool near-white ground with a whisper-soft sage top wash, translucent frosted-white panels with 1px hairlines and diffuse soft shadows, near-black `#0A0C0C` ink, `#5B6560` secondary ink, single pitch-green accent `#098558` reserved strictly for live/striker/4/6 states, and refined red `#D74956` reserved strictly for wickets. Tight geometric grotesk with tabular numerals; light-weight runs paired with semibold `/wickets`.
- **STORY:** A scorer standing in sunlight glances once to confirm score, overs, chase math, batters, and bowler, then records any delivery in one thumb tap with instant haptic feedback and one-tap undo.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Top bar (back, live dot + match title + format pill, undo); open monumental score `142/3` left + chase stack right + overs below; two side-by-side frosted batter pills (striker with 1px green hairline); centered bowler line; horizontal over timeline (faded previous over + current over's 6 circles + dismissal label on wicket); bottom 45% raised frosted-glass sheet with grab handle holding a 3×2 keypad (`0 1 2 / 3 4 6` with green 4 & 6), 4-segment extras row (`Wide | No ball | Bye | Leg bye`), and red `Wicket` button beside `Run out?`.
- **FORM:** Daylight Glass (Composition B: Open score + glass bottom sheet), canon visual direction adapted to user standing preference, seed key `3129d1e8`.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
